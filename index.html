✅ Station inventory

19 Bulacan PDRRMO-documented monitoring points are loaded with coordinates and bridge/river locations.

✅ Field/manual observations

Authorized volunteers can enter a level, timestamp, source and status. The app stores the latest and previous observation locally and calculates the rate of rise.

⏳ PhilSensors connector

The production version should use an approved DOST-ASTI API/data-sharing route. Until then, the dashboard deliberately shows NO DATA instead of pretending to be live.
